# Name check for the fictional company (2026-09-29)

The case-study company must not match a real firm. Each candidate was web-searched with its industry words.

| candidate | query | found | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Ridge Equipment | "Kestrel Ridge Equipment" manufacturer | several real "Kestrel" equipment brands (Kestrel Equipment Ltd, Kestrel Instruments, a Kestrel hedgecutter model) | rejected: too close to real equipment brands |
| Tamarack Fluid Systems | "Tamarack Fluid Systems" industrial equipment | Tamarack Industries, a real heating-equipment manufacturer; Tamarack Products, a real equipment maker | rejected |
| Larchmere Pumpworks | "Larchmere Pumpworks" | PumpWorks, a real pump manufacturer in Houston | rejected: "Pumpworks" is taken |
| **Fenwold Machine Works** | "Fenwold" manufacturer industrial equipment; "Fenwold Machine Works" | no manufacturer; the only business named Fenwold is a veterinary practice in England, a different field | **chosen** |

Control: a nonsense string ("zqxv flormbat quenidrate") also returned unrelated links, so the search tool never
returns nothing. A verdict rests on what the results were, not on whether results came back.

## Second check: a new name for a new product line (2026-09-30)

The company's products changed from pumps to truck-mounted tanks, drums and containers, so it needed a name that fits
them. Candidates were checked with the same method. One further candidate, proposed during the work, was rejected as a
video-game brand also used by real manufacturing firms; that record is kept with the project's private notes.

| candidate | query | found | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ridgecast Industries | "Ridgecast Industries" | Ridge Cast Metals Corporation, a real industrial-products firm in California | rejected: too close to a real metals firm |
| Harlow Metalworks | "Harlow Metalworks" | The Harlow Group, a real precision sheet-metal fabricator in Harlow, England | rejected |
| **Tanmark Industries** | "Tanmark Industries" | no manufacturer; an entertainment agency, a belt retailer, a dissolved UK company, and TanMar Companies (oil and gas equipment, spelled differently) | **chosen** (2026-09-30) |

Control: an exact nonsense name ("Qzvrmblt Industries") returned only loosely similar names (QZ Industries, Q
Industries) and no exact match, while the rejected candidates returned exact matches. So the tool can tell an exact hit
from a near one.
